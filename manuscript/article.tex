the methodological foundation for this implementation \citep{Loh:2002}.
\pkg{guide-rs} implements a subset of GUIDE-style methods in a
\proglang{Rust} library, with a command-line interface for statistical
analysis and automation. A complementary \proglang{Python} wrapper makes
the implementation accessible through familiar estimator workflows.
